# Look Development — Generated Reference Frames

Seven frames generated to test the art direction against a real image model
before committing to a full render. They cover all three acts and the key
emotional beats.

**Model:** Z Image (Higgsfield) · 2048×1152 · 16:9 · 0.15 credits each
**Total spend:** 1.05 credits (the account's full remaining balance)

## Why these seven

Each frame was chosen to stress-test a specific risk in the brief:

| # | Shot | What it tests |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | `S1-02` | The protagonist's visual anchor — the single most important consistency asset |
| 1 | `OPEN-01` | Whether the campus reads as Sri Lankan rather than generic |
| 2 | `S2-03` | The "not Silicon Valley" rule, in the hardest room to get right |
| 3 | `S7-01` | Whether the mathematics scene can be beautiful rather than dry |
| 4 | `S10-04` | Whether the integrity beat reads as empathetic, not punitive |
| 5 | `S13-04` | Quantum hardware with wonder but without overclaiming |
| 6 | `FIN-02` | The payoff composition — campus behind, technology ahead |

## Notes for the full render

These were generated with a **fast, budget** model chosen to fit the remaining
balance — they establish composition, palette and mood, not final quality. For
the actual film:

- Use a character-identity model (Soul Cast, or a trained reusable Soul) for the
  41 shots featuring the student. Text-only generation will not hold a
  consistent face across 41 shots, which is the failure mode most likely to sink
  the film.
- Generate the character sheet **first** and pass it as an image reference to
  every subsequent shot.
- These seven frames are usable as style references to keep the full render
  anchored to the approved look.
