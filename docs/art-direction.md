# Art Direction Bible

**You Enter University to Study Computer Science — But Are You Ready for the Future?**

This document governs every shot. Where it disagrees with a generation prompt,
this document wins.

---

## 1. The core idea

The film is a **warning, a roadmap and a motivator** — in that order, and never
a recruitment advert for a degree. The student is never mocked, the university
is never criticised, and no technology is ever shown as dead.

The emotional line is a single arc:

> *excited and unaware* → *shown the scale of the change* → *given the method* →
> *capable and unafraid*

---

## 2. The three-act look

The visual language must migrate, never jump. Every act inherits the previous
one's warmth; the cool palette is **added**, not substituted.

### Act A — Grounded (`OPEN`, `S1`, `S2`) · 00:00 – 01:11

Recognisably Sri Lankan. This is the anchor the rest of the film departs from,
and it is the single most important thing to get right — if Act A reads as a
generic Western campus, the whole film loses its meaning.

**Must have:** red clay-tiled roofs, whitewashed colonnades and pillared open
corridors, ceiling fans, louvred windows, tropical light shafts with visible
dust, rain-trees and palms, worn wooden fold-down lecture seats, chalk-dusted
whiteboards, paper noticeboards layered many deep, older desktop tower PCs
alongside newer flat screens.

**Must not have:** glass-and-steel towers, open-plan tech campuses, beanbags,
neon, snow, deciduous autumn trees, Western signage.

`Warm ochre #C98A3C · terracotta #A9502F · foliage green #2F5D3A · chalk white #F2EDE3 · sky #9FC7DE`

### Act B — Hybrid (`S3`–`S9`) · 01:11 – 04:28

The campus does not disappear. Translucent cyan data layers grow **inside** the
real rooms — a holographic timeline pierces the lecture hall wall, concept
solids orbit above a real desk. Physical and digital share every frame.

`Act A palette + holo cyan #4FD8E8 · signal amber #F2A93B · deep teal #0E3B45`

### Act C — Global and futuristic (`S10`–`FINAL`) · 04:28 – 07:54

Deep indigo voids, volumetric light, suspended geometry. Futuristic but never
sterile or cold — there is always one warm light source, and it is usually the
student. Warmth fully returns in the final sunrise, where both palettes meet.

`Deep indigo #131A3A · quantum violet #7A5CF0 · holo cyan #4FD8E8 · sunrise gold #F5B95C`

## 3. The protagonist

**Tharindu**, 19, first-year Computer Science undergraduate. Referred to as
*they/them* in all production documents.

> Warm brown skin, short black wavy hair, thin silver-rimmed glasses, pale blue
> short-sleeve collared shirt, dark navy canvas backpack, simple white canvas shoes.

Use this description **verbatim** in every prompt featuring them, and drive
every such shot from a single character-sheet reference image. Without a locked
reference the face will drift between shots and the film falls apart.

**The arc is played in the body, not the wardrobe.** Act A: shoulders raised,
gripping the backpack strap, gaze darting. Act C: shoulders down, hands open,
gaze level and steady. Same person, same clothes, different bearing.

---

## 4. Rules that are not negotiable

These come directly from the brief and are the difference between a good film
and one that misleads students.

1. **No technology is ever shown as dead.** Java's emblem in `S4-03` stays fully
   lit and intact while the interface around it changes. Never dim it, crack it,
   grey it out, bury it or let it fall. The whole point of the scene is that the
   *concepts* persist — the shot fails if the older language reads as a casualty.
2. **Theory is never the poor relation.** In `S8-01` the stone walkway and the
   cyan lattice walkway are equally grand and equally lit. Never light theory as
   dusty, cobwebbed or abandoned.
3. **The plagiarism beat is empathetic, not punitive.** `S10-04` is framed as a
   trap the student fell into, not a crime. Dawning realisation, never
   humiliation. No invigilators looming, no red stamps, no disciplinary imagery.
4. **Quantum computing is presented as uncertain.** `S13` uses conditional
   language throughout ("still emerging", "if it reaches the level where…").
   Never imply quantum computing has already arrived or will certainly deliver.
5. **"Future — Unknown" is bright, not black.** `S12-03` ends the timeline at an
   open field of warm light. Uncertainty is framed as opportunity, never dread.
6. **No readable brand marks.** Noticeboards, screens and UI carry abstract
   placeholder text only. No invented institution names, no logos.
7. **On-screen text stays minimal.** Never more than can be read comfortably at
   the shot's duration. Dense label groups reveal in waves, never all at once.

## 5. Camera

One continuous grammar, accelerating and then settling:

| Act | Movement | Feel |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Slow dollies, gentle arcs, one locked static per scene | Observational, patient |
| B | Tracking and orbiting, speed climbing scene to scene | Propulsive, curious |
| C | Cranes, scale shifts, one abrupt stop (`S12-03`) | Expansive, then still |

Two deliberate stillness beats break the acceleration and carry the most
important ideas — **`S7` (mathematics)** drops to almost no motion, and
**`FIN-06`** is fully locked. Both are intentional; do not add movement.

**The visual rhyme:** `FIN-01` reuses the exact framing of `OPEN-02` / `S1-01`
at golden hour, with the student walking out instead of in. This is the
emotional payoff of the whole film — match the angle precisely.

---

## 6. Audio

A single evolving orchestral-electronic cue, not a playlist.

- **Act A** — solo piano, light strings, subtle hand percussion.
- **Act B** — pulse and arpeggios enter, tempo climbs with the timeline.
- **Act C** — full strings and synth pads.
- **`S10`** — strip back to near silence for the plagiarism beat. The silence is
  the point.
- **`FINAL`** — resolve to warm major; a single sustained piano note over the end card.

Ambience: birds, distant chatter, ceiling fans, chalk, keyboards. Holographic
whooshes on act transitions, low sub-bass on act changes.

**Mix:** narration peaks at −3 dB; music ducks to −18 dB under narration and
comes up full at transitions.
